Arts and foundation websites

Masterwork Music and Art Foundation’s current redesign brief joins awards, video and audio, giving, a secure Board area, Mailchimp, Blogger, and social connections in one modest-budget rebuild. That combination makes staff ownership—not just the initial design—the deciding scope question.

Event and ticketing websites

A short launch window makes ownership decisions urgent.

A current event-promoter brief asks for a polished multi-page site, online ticket sales, and an end-of-month launch. Before design begins, the first phase should settle who owns ticket inventory, checkout, attendee data, confirmations, refunds, and the door-entry fallback.

Choose the ticket modelCompare a hosted ticket page, embedded checkout, and custom flow by fees, inventory, attendee-data access, refunds, and operating effort.
Design the mobile pathMake event details, ticket choices, checkout, confirmation, and venue information easy to reach and operate on a phone.
Plan the door fallbackName the check-in owner, confirmation lookup, duplicate-order response, connectivity fallback, and launch acceptance test.

Visual portfolio and studio websites

Make the work vivid without making the site slow or invisible.

A current creative-studio request joins website development with SEO. For a media-rich portfolio, the first phase should define which work earns a landing page, how each asset stays discoverable and accessible, and which performance budget protects the mobile inquiry path.

Structure the portfolioGive priority services and projects crawlable pages, useful surrounding copy, stable URLs, and a clear route to inquiry.
Describe each assetMatch filenames, captions, alt text, and nearby context to what the image communicates; keep decorative images out of the reading experience.
Set a media budgetDefine responsive sources, dimensions, formats, loading priority, and acceptance checks for the largest mobile visuals.
